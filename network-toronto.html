What this is

Not a networking event. A club that happens to be useful.

Most rooms sell you a ticket and let you fend for yourself. We seat you.

Principle 01Seating

Every seat is assigned

Never beside a competitor, never beside the person you came with.

Principle 02Scale

Small on purpose

Dinners cap at twelve. We grow the calendar, not the room.

Principle 03Discretion

Nothing leaves the table

No photos, no press, no recording. Live deals get discussed here.

Principle 04Reciprocity

Give before you take

We track introductions made, not nights attended.

Questions

Before you apply.

Do I need to know a member already?

It helps, but no. A third joined without one — we seat those applicants at First Table.

Is this a pitch event?

Pitching from a stage is banned. Pitching over dinner is what dinner is for.

What's the real time commitment?

Eight or nine evenings a year. Fewer than three and it stops working.

Can I bring a guest?

Two passes a quarter, and guests are welcome at First Table. Twice, then they apply.

Are dues refundable?

Refundable pro rata for sixty days. After that the rooms are booked.
